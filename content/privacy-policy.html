--- title: Privacy Policy description: How Essential Music Theory handles your information. section: page ---

This site is run by Simon Horsey. It is designed to collect as little information about you as possible.

What this site does not do

  • No advertising networks.
  • No advertising or social media tracking pixels.
  • No selling or sharing of your details with anyone.

Visitor statistics

The site counts page views using privacy-friendly analytics that do not use cookies and do not identify you.

Buying the book

Essential Music Theory is sold through Apple Books. Your purchase is between you and Apple, and I never see your name, email address or card details.

Courses

If you enrol in a course, the course platform stores your name and email address so you can log in and so I can help with any problems. Payments are handled by the platform's payment provider. I never see your card details.

Videos

A few lessons include a video hosted on YouTube. They are embedded in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, which means YouTube does not set cookies unless you play the video.

Contact form

Messages sent through the contact form are used only to reply to you.

Links to other sites

Some pages link to products on other sites, and some of those are affiliate links: if you buy, I may earn a small commission at no cost to you. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Questions

If you want to know what information I hold about you, or want it deleted, contact me.
