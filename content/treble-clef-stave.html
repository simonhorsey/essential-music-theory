what does the treble clef written at the beginning of a stave tell us about the notes written on that stave?

Asked by Aashley (Taree NSW)

Answers

Simon Horsey · Nov 10, 2015

Hi Ashley,

The treble clef tells us the location of the note G, hence it is also called the G clef.

So the treble clef shows us that G comes on the second line as that is the line which the clef is curled around.

Hope this helps!

Simon
