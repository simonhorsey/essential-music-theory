A music theory workbook does at some point become necessary. It is all very well completing the reviews inside Essential Music Theory and having them marked immediately, but in an exam you have to write the music down on paper. That is where the Essential Music Theory Activity Book comes in.

The Activity Book contains activities and questions linked to each chapter of Essential Music Theory, Volume 1. You can choose a version for younger students or one for older students and adults. The content and questions are basically the same, with a few differences.

Activity Book 1 for younger children

Aimed at students up to about 12. It is enjoyable to use, and still makes sure they have the understanding they need for Grade 1 and Grade 2 theory.

  • Friendly cover
  • Child-friendly activities and questions
  • Child-friendly font (not Comic Sans!)
  • Slightly bigger music staves
  • Extra music reading activities, and help with drawing notes at the start
  • Links directly to the chapters in the book

Activity Book 1 for older children and adults

Aimed at older students and adults, with some optional extension activities.

  • Interesting activities and questions
  • Extension music reading activities, so everybody has a firm foundation in this key skill
  • Links directly to the chapters in the book, so it is easy to look something up
  • Everything you need to help you pass Grade 1 and Grade 2 theory
