What can you use to remember ACEG on the F clef?

Asked by Valerie (Nigeria)

Answers

Simon Horsey · Jan 06, 2015

Remembering notes on the F clef

Hi,

The traditional method is All Cows Eat Grass, but if you learn

A C E G B D F

(All Cows Eat Grass But Don't Fly) you can use it for all the lines and spaces in the F and G clef.. and the C clef later on as well!
