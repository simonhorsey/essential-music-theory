Counting 5/4 timing

5/4 can be counted in 2 different ways, depending on the phrasing of the piece of music.

You can either count

1, 2, 3, 4, 5

or

1, 2, 3, 4, 5

where the bold numbers have a slight accent.

In some pieces the accent may change in the middle!
