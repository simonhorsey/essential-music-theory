What is an ostinato bass?

Answers

Simon Horsey · Aug 08, 2017

Ostinato

An ostinato is a repeating pattern. So an ostinato bass is a repeating pattern in the bass. This could be something as simple as a 2 note pattern (C - G - C - G) or a more complex, longer pattern (C - E - G - A - C - E - G - A etc.)
