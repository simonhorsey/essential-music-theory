Hi Donald,

Without seeing the score I can't be sure, but I would assume that the piano takes the bottom 2 staves and the guitar the top staff.

You can check because the piano will have a bass clef, so this will be the bottom staff. The staff above that will also be piano. The other one is guitar!

Hope that helped!
