Hello, I'm Simon Horsey. Welcome to Essential Music Theory.

I have taught music for more than 20 years, to whole classes and to one student at a time, from seven-year-olds to adults. In that time I have met a great many people who believe music theory is hard, and who will go to almost any lengths to avoid learning to read music.

It isn't hard. Not if you go about it the right way.

Why I built this site

Teaching the same ideas year after year, I began to see that nearly everybody gets stuck in the same few places: what a dot does to a note, how to work out a key signature, why some intervals are "perfect" and others aren't. Once you know where the stumbling blocks are, you can show people the way round them.

So I started writing the explanations down, first as lessons on this site and then as a book you can listen to as well as read: Essential Music Theory, Volume 1. Every one of my theory students has passed their exam, most with a Merit or a Distinction, and they all learned it the way the book teaches it.

As for theory being fun... well, quite a few people argue with me about that. But once they see how the ideas fit together, most agree it can be. Not as much fun as Disney World, maybe. But still fun.

What you will find here

  • The book, which takes you from complete beginner to Grade 2, with Volumes 2 and 3 on the way.
  • The courses: a Beginner Course, then one for each grade up to Grade 5.
  • Free lessons and answers to readers' questions.

I also run Essential Music Practice, which is about how to practice so that it actually works. Understanding your music and practicing it well go together.

If you have a question, get in touch. I do my best to reply to every message.

Simon
