Thank you !

Asked by Chriss (Arizona)

Answers

Simon Horsey · Dec 08, 2016

Triplet

I think you mean a triplet.

This is technically three notes in the time of two - but three Eighth Notes in the time of one Quarter Note fits the description since one Quarter Note = 2 Eighth Notes.
