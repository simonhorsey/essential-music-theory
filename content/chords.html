Answers

Simon Horsey · Jul 03, 2016

If it is iv you are looking that would be the chord if F minor. For IV it is F major - just checking the upper lower case. This is because F is the 4th note of C major and the chord is built on that. In general lower case denotes minor chords and upper case major chords.

Hope this helps!
