Music Theory Workbook

A music theory workbook does at some point become necessary. It's all very well completing the reviews in the Essentials For Music Theory iBook and having them marked immediately, but in an exam you have to actually write the music down on paper and that is where the Essential Music Theory Activity Book comes in. This music theory workbook contains activities and questions linked to the chapters of the Essentials For Music Theory book, no matter which version you have, iBook, kindle, nook or PDF, the chapters are all the same so all relate to the Activity Book. 

You can even choose whether you want a book aimed at younger students or older students/adults. The content and questions are basically the same, but with a few differences as outlined below.


Essential Music Theory Activity Book 1
For Younger Children

This version of the book is aimed at younger students aged up to about 12. it has a the following features that make it enjoyable for the students to use but still ensure they have the understanding they need to pass the Grade 1 and 2 ABRSM Theory exams if they are taking them.

  • Friendly cover
  • Child friendly activities and questions
  • Child friendly font (not comic sans!)
  • Slightly bigger music staves
  • Links directly to chapters in the Essentials For Music Theory Book.
  • Contains extra music reading activities and help with drawing notes at the start
  • Everything you need to help you pass Grade 1 and 2 music theory exams (ABRSM)



Essential Music Theory Activity Book 1
For Older Children and Adults

This version of the book is aimed at older students and adults. It has the following features to make sure learning and understanding music theory is fun and also includes some optional extension activities. 

  • Interesting activities and questions to engage students
  • Links directly to chapters in the Essentials For Music Theory Book to make referencing questions and ensuring understanding of key concepts easier
  • Contains some extension music reading activities to ensure all students have a firm foundation in this key skill
  • Everything you need to help you pass Grade 1 and 2 music theory exams (ABRSM)
